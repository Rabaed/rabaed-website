# 75: The Footer directory, so every page can be reached from the foot of every page

**What to build:** The footer of every page, Arabic and English, gets a **Footer directory** (CONTEXT.md): four columns of links through which every page of the Marketing site can be reached, including the pages the header has no room for. Today the footer links only to Terms and Privacy. The blog is linked from nowhere on the site, the Pour Tracker page only from a teaser on the start page, and the Referral Terms only from the referral page. Editors can change the columns in the CMS.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**The founder sees it before it merges.** The pull request is not merged until they have opened its preview deployment on their desktop and their phone, looked at the footer in both languages, and said yes to the column headings and link words.

- [ ] Every Arabic page's footer shows the four columns below, in this order, with these links

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement (a deliberate departure from the Reference site)
**Summary:** Replace the footer's two legal links with a four-column Footer directory reaching every page, editable by an Editor.

**The word:** the founder asked for "the sitemap at the end of the website". In this project a sitemap is `/sitemap.xml`, the file search engines read, and it already lists every page. What the founder means is the columns of links at the foot of the page: the **Footer directory**, now in CONTEXT.md. Keep the two apart in code, CMS labels and tests.

**Current behaviour (confirmed 23 September 2026):**

- The footer is the wordmark, the tagline, five social icons, then one wrapping row of links and the rights line. It is the Reference site's footer exactly; every `reference/site/*.html` page has the same one, with no columns.
- The links come from the Site-wide words entry, Footer tab: a list named for terms and privacy links, at most 4 rows, each a label and a path (ticket 59). It has two rows, Terms and Privacy.
- The header is full (ADR-0015, spec: the header is full at the words it launched with). The blog was deliberately left out of the navigation by ticket 23 until the founder chose where to link it from. This ticket is that choice.
- Customer stories are hidden, link and all, until the first case study is published, per language. The header filters its link by path; the footer has no such filter because it has no such link.
- Terms, Privacy and Referral Terms are Arabic only. The English footer's links lead to a page that only says so.

**Desired behaviour (decided by the founder, 23 September 2026):**

The four columns. The Arabic words are proposals the founder approves in the preview; the link words reuse what the site already calls each page.

| Column | Arabic | English | Links, in order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rabaed | ربائد | Rabaed | Home, Product, Get started, Pour Tracker |
| Programmes | البرامج | Programs | Referral Program, Partnership Program |
| Resources | المصادر | Resources | Blog, Customer stories |
| Legal | قانوني | Legal | Terms and conditions, Privacy policy, Referral Terms |

- **Individual articles and case studies are not listed**, only the Blog and Customer stories index pages.
- **Customer stories hides itself.** Any footer link whose path is the Customer stories index is dropped while that language has no published case study, by the same check the header uses, so an Editor never has to remember the rule.
- **English Legal links go straight to the Arabic pages**, labelled with the language, for example "Terms and conditions (Arabic)". Mark the links as Arabic-language destinations. The founder chose this over linking to the "Arabic only" notice page: one tap fewer, and the label is honest.
- **An Editor's.** Columns and links are CMS content, in the same Site-wide words entry, Footer tab: up to 4 columns, each a heading and up to 6 links, each link a label and a path, as the spec already requires of every menu or footer link. This follows the founder's earlier choice that a page added later can be linked without a developer.
- **Phone layout:** two columns by two rows below 981px. Desktop shows the four side by side.
- **English words are a draft for the founder**, like the rest of the English site words: seed them unpublished.

**The ADR:** the Reference site's footer has no columns; this adds a directory it has none of. It says why (pages reachable from nowhere, a header with no room), what an Editor can change and the limits, and the English Legal choice. Follow ADR-0013 and ADR-0015: narrow the footer comparison by name rather than drop it. ADR numbers collide between parallel sessions; take the next free number and renumber at merge if another lane took it.

**Tests to expect to change:** every page's visual baselines (the footer is on all of them), and the footer comparisons against the Reference site at every width. Keep what still matches (wordmark, tagline, icons, rights line) held exactly, and give up only the footer's height and the link row, by name. The CMS change needs a migration that moves existing Terms and Privacy rows into the Legal column. A preview deployment built before that migration has run needs redeploying before the founder looks at it.
