# 76: The mobile menu puts Login and the language on one row, and dims the page behind it

**What to build:** Two changes to the menu panel that opens from the header below 1100px.

- **One row.** Login and the language switcher sit side by side on one row under the menu links. Login is a compact outlined button on one side (right in Arabic, left in English) and the language link is on the other. Today they are stacked: a full-width outlined Login pill, then «English» on a row of its own. The founder's screenshot circles this as looking wrong.
- **A dimmed page behind.** While the panel is open, the page below it is dimmed and does not scroll, and tapping the dimmed part closes the panel. Today the panel stops partway down the screen, the page shows through underneath at full strength (the hero line and the «60 يوماً» badge in the founder's screenshot), it scrolls, and tapping it does nothing.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement (a deliberate departure from the Reference site)
**Summary:** In the mobile menu panel, put Login and the language switcher on one row, and dim, lock and make tappable the page behind the open panel.

**Current behaviour (confirmed 23 September 2026):**

- The panel is a dropdown hanging from the fixed header, not a full-screen layer. It opens to at most 590px or the window's height minus the header, whichever is smaller. On an 812px-tall phone about 190px of page shows below it, unchanged.
- It stacks everything in one column. Login is last but one: a full-width outlined pill with a 14px gap above it, which is the Reference site's own Login rule exactly. The language switcher comes after it (ADR-0015 put it in the panel) and inherits every panel link's padding and top border, so it reads as a menu link. On a page with no translation a small note follows it.
- No dimming, no scroll lock, and tapping outside the panel does not close it. The Reference site is the same, with a shorter panel.
- Login's address is one CMS value for both languages. **Out of scope:** the founder chose on 23 September 2026 that Arabic and English pages keep sending visitors to the same sign-in address.

**Desired behaviour (decided by the founder, 23 September 2026):**

- **The row:** Login and language on one line, on opposite sides, mirrored between Arabic and English as the rest of the site is. Login is a compact outlined button, the desktop header's Login style, not the full-width pill. The language link gets a small globe icon and stops looking like a menu link.
- **The note** stays, under the row. ADR-0015 chose the panel partly because it has room for this note, so it must not be lost.
- **The panel stays a panel.** The founder chose this over a full-screen menu. The page behind is dimmed, cannot scroll while the panel is open, and a tap on it closes the panel.
- **Unchanged:** the links, the Partnerships group, the panel's 1100px breakpoint (ADR-0015), and every way it closes today.

**The ADR:** the Reference site's panel ends with a full-width Login and has no language switcher, no dimming and no tap-to-close. This records the one-row layout and the dimmed page as deliberate, with the founder's reason (the stacked Login and language looked wrong on a phone, and the page showing through read as unfinished). It follows ADR-0013 and ADR-0015. ADR numbers collide between parallel sessions; take the next free number and renumber at merge if another lane took it.

**Tests to expect to change:** the shell's Reference comparison of the panel's Login, which today gives up only its left edge and width; it now also gives up its height and row, recorded by name. The page-shell test that the panel opens unclipped with Login visible, and the localisation test for the no-translation note, should keep passing; adjust only what the new row moves.
