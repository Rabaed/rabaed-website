# 72: The calculator's thumb reaches both ends of its bar, and the orange fill ends under it

**What to build:** On the home page's delay-cost calculator, each slider's round thumb and its orange fill move together. Wherever the thumb is, the fill ends exactly under the middle of it. At the lowest value the thumb sits at the left end of the bar with no orange showing past it. At the highest value it sits at the right end, with the bar orange all the way. Today they only line up at the halfway point: near the start the thumb runs ahead of an empty or short fill, and at the end the thumb stops short of the bar's right edge. The bar keeps the thickness, colours and rounded ends it has now. Arabic and English home pages both.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug (inherited from the Reference site; the founder calls the fix an enhancement)
**Summary:** The fill and the thumb measure the bar differently, so they agree only at 50%. Take away the bar's side padding so the thumb travels its full length, and work the fill out along the thumb's travel rather than the bar's full width.

**Current behaviour (confirmed 23 September 2026, founder's screenshots):**

- The sliders pick up the calculator's rule for text fields, a rule the Reference site wrote for inputs its calculator no longer has. It gives each slider 12px × 14px padding and a 1px border, which is what makes the bar a thick pill rather than the 6px line the slider's own rule asks for.
- The fill is a gradient painted across the whole bar, padding included, at `(value − min) / (max − min)` of its width.
- The thumb only travels inside the padding, and its centre can come no nearer an end than half its own width. So its centre runs from about 26px in to about 26px short of each end.
- At the minimum (the project-value slider's starting 1,000,000 in the founder's screenshot) the fill is empty and the thumb sits about 26px in. At the maximum (48 months in the second screenshot) the fill reaches the end and the thumb stops about 26px short. The Reference site does the same on its home page.

**Desired behaviour (decided by the founder, 23 September 2026):**

- **Option A′:** keep the bar's thickness; remove only the side padding, so the thumb reaches both ends. The founder chose this over thinning the bar to a 6px line (a bigger visual change) and over only moving the fill (which would leave the thumb short of the right end).
- The fill ends under the thumb's centre at every value, so at the minimum it is hidden behind the thumb and at the maximum it fills the bar.
- The fill is worked out in one place, which both the server's starting drawing and the browser's redraw already share. Keep it that way.
- **Wording:** the founder asked that this be called an enhancement over the Reference site, fixing a defect it has, not "a deliberate divergence". It still has to be written in the code, so a later session does not put the padding back to match `reference/`.

**Tests to expect to change:**

- The home page's Reference comparison measures the slider's box: its side padding now differs from the Reference site's. Record it as an allowance naming this ticket, not by dropping the slider from the comparison.
- The comparison that moves the sliders on both sites and compares their track colouring will now differ: the fill stops are computed along the thumb's travel. Compare the words and figures as before, and hold the fill to the thumb in its own test rather than to the Reference site's gradient.

No ADR: the change is easy to reverse and its reason is plain, so a comment where the rule lives covers it.
