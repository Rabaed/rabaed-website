# 71: Big screens stop adding empty space — the hero shares the first screen with the logos, and section gaps stop growing at 72px

**What to build:** On a tall desktop screen the site stops filling itself with empty space. Opening the home page shows the hero and, under it, the whole Trust strip, without scrolling, on any desktop screen tall enough to hold both. The hero is never taller than 860px, however big the monitor. The space above and below every section, on every page, stops growing at 72px instead of 96px, so where two sections meet the gap is at most 144px instead of 192px. Phones, short desktop windows and the Record section behave exactly as they do now.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement (a deliberate departure from the Reference site)
**Summary:** Two vertical measures in the Reference site grow with the screen. Cap both, so a big monitor stops adding empty space, and let the hero leave room for the Trust strip in the first screen.

**Current behaviour (confirmed 23 September 2026):**

- The home **hero** is exactly the window's height (never less than 760px on desktop), with its content centred in it. The content does not grow with the window, so the empty space above and below it does, with no ceiling. At 1920×1080 that is about 200px above and about 200px below the content. Below 981px wide the hero is already sized by its content; desktop windows 700px tall or less have their own rule. Both stay as they are.
- The shared **section spacing** is 8% of the window's height, never less than 56px and never more than 96px, above and below each section. Two sections meeting give up to 192px of gap. It is the Reference site's own value (it is in `reference/HANDOFF.md`), carried over exactly.

**Desired behaviour (decided by the founder, 23 September 2026):**

- **Hero:** height is the window's height *minus the Trust strip's height*, never more than **860px**, never less than the current 760px floor. The founder's reason: on their 1080px screen, 860px leaves the logos of the companies involved on the first screen. Taking the strip's height off the window makes that true on every desktop screen tall enough, not just at 1080px. When the Trust strip is switched off, there is nothing to leave room for: the hero is the window's height, capped at 860px. The strip's height should be the height it actually renders at on desktop (one row that moves, ticket 06), not a figure the strip could quietly outgrow.
- **Section spacing:** the ceiling drops from 96px to **72px**. The floor (56px) and the 8%-of-height rate stay. It is one shared value, so every page follows. The pages that use a multiple of it (the article list, an article, the legal pages) follow in proportion, and that is intended.
- **Out of scope:** the Record section. It fills the window while it is pinned, which is how its scroll effect works. The founder chose to leave it alone.

**The ADR:** it overrules the Reference site's `--sp` ceiling and its full-window hero, so a later reader comparing the build against `reference/` does not "fix" them back. It says both measures grew with the screen and left big monitors mostly empty. It says the hero now shares the first screen with the Trust strip, and gives the two numbers (860px, 72px). It should also say that the section spacing is unchanged at every height the Reference comparisons are captured at. The ADR numbering collides between parallel sessions; take the next free number and renumber at merge if another lane took it.

**Tests to expect to change:**

- **Section spacing:** none. Every Reference comparison is captured at 900px tall or less, where 8% of the height is already 72px or less.
- **Hero:** the Reference comparisons at desktop widths (981px and up) at 900px and 840px tall. There the hero is now shorter than the Reference site's, and its centred content sits higher. Record it as a deliberate difference, the way the whole-page comparison already lists other sections that are deliberately a different height: name this ticket and the ADR, and state the allowance, rather than dropping the comparison. At 700px tall and below, and below 981px wide, the hero's comparisons should not change.
