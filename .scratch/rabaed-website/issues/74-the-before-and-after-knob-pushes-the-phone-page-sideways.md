# 74: Bug — the before-and-after knob pushes a phone's page sideways

**What is wrong:** On a phone, the home page's before-and-after comparison («قبل وبعد», `#ba`) can make the whole page slide a few pixels sideways. The comparison's round orange knob is 52px across and sits centred on the seam. When the seam reaches the comparison's left end, half the knob hangs past it — and on screens up to about 560px wide the comparison is only 20px from the edge of the screen, so 6px of the knob sticks out past the screen itself. The page is then 6px wider than the screen, and a sideways swipe moves it. The spec promises the opposite: "every section to work vertically with no sideways scrolling" on a 360px phone (user story 4).

It happens two ways:

- **On its own, once per visit:** the one-time hint sweeps the seam to the left end, pauses, and comes back. For about half a second the page is up to 6px too wide.
- **When the visitor drags the seam to the left end:** the page stays 6px too wide (8px while the knob is held, since it grows) until they move it back.

The Reference site does exactly the same, to the pixel. It is not a Reference-site behaviour worth keeping: nothing in the Reference site or in `reference/HANDOFF.md` asks for it, and the Reference site's own guard — `overflow-x: clip` on the page — is there to stop exactly this. It doesn't stop it here.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** needs-triage — the founder picks how the knob should look at the end of its travel (see "Desired behaviour"); then ready-for-agent

- [ ] At 360px, 390px, 430px and 560px wide, with motion on, the page never becomes wider than the screen while the hint runs
- [ ] Dragging the seam all the way to either end, or stepping it there with the arrow keys, never makes the page wider than the screen, while held or after letting go
- [ ] On a touch phone, a sideways swipe over the page never moves it while the hint runs
- [ ] The comparison, the knob and the seam look as they do now at every width, apart from what the founder chooses below
- [ ] The same on the English home page, where the hint sweeps the other way and the knob sticks out on the right
- [ ] The code names this as an enhancement over the Reference site (a defect it has, fixed), so a later session does not undo it to match `reference/`
- [ ] A test samples the page's width over the first ~3 seconds after the comparison comes into view at 390px, and fails today

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug (inherited from the Reference site)
**Summary:** The before-and-after comparison's 52px knob hangs 6px past the screen's left edge whenever the seam reaches the left end at widths up to 560px, which widens the page; clip it at the section's edge as `#pain` and `#proof` already are, or keep the knob inside.

**Current behaviour (confirmed 23 September 2026, built app and Reference site side by side, Playwright Chromium):**

- **The part:** `.cmp-handle .grip` (`src/styles/home.css`, the `.cmp-handle` rules): 52px round, centred on the seam with `translate(-50%, -50%)`, `scale(1.09)` on hover or while dragging. The seam is `left: var(--p)` on `.cmp-handle`, and `--p` runs 0–100% (`clampSeam` in `src/components/home/before-after-seam.ts`). At 0% the knob's centre is on `.cmp`'s left edge.
- **The room:** at 360–560px, `.cmp` spans 20px → (width − 20px). The knob's half-width is 26px, so it reaches −6px (−8.3px scaled). From 700px up the gutter is 32px and nothing sticks out.

  | Width | Seam at the left end: page too wide by |
  | --- | --- |
  | 360, 390, 430, 560px | 8px (knob held) — 6px at rest |
  | 700, 768, 1024px | 0 |

  Identical on the Reference site at every width.
- **The animation:** the hint in `src/components/home/before-after-behaviour.tsx` starts when `#ba`'s top reaches 62% down the window, waits 0.3s, sets the seam to 100%, sweeps it to 0% over 1.8s (`power2.inOut`), pauses 0.35s, and returns to 50% over 0.8s. Sampled every frame at 390×900 after scrolling `#ba` into view: 0 until ~2.2s, then 1px rising to 6px as the sweep eases into the left end, 6px through the pause, back to 0 at ~2.85s. The Reference site's curve is the same, about 50ms earlier.
- **Why only the left:** the page is right to left, so extra width on the right (the knob at 100% reaches 397px on a 390px screen) is clipped and can't be scrolled to; extra width on the left can. On the English page the sweep ends at the right, so the mirror case applies — not checked, since the test database has only a draft English home page.
- **Can a visitor feel it:** with a mouse, no — a sideways wheel leaves `scrollX` at 0, because `body { overflow-x: clip }` reaches the viewport as `hidden`. On a touch phone (Playwright `isMobile`, `hasTouch`), yes: during the hint `scrollX` read −2 to −4 before any input, and −6 after a sideways swipe, on both sites. It returns to 0 when the knob comes back.
- **Why earlier searches missed it:** they listed elements whose right edge passed the window's width. The knob sticks out on the *left*: its left edge goes negative. Clipping each element in turn (`overflow: clip`) and watching `scrollWidth` points to `.cmp-handle`'s child, the knob, and nothing else. The `span.grip` seen once at right=392.9 is the same knob at the sweep's starting point, 100%, which is on the clipped side. Nothing overflows while the page sits at the top, because the hint only runs once `#ba` is on screen; the calculator test scrolls past `#ba` on its way to the sliders, which is why it sees this.

**Desired behaviour (for the founder to choose):**

- **Option A (recommended): clip it at the section's edge.** Add `overflow-x: clip` to `#ba`, as `#pain` (the card decks) and `#proof` already have, with a comment in the same voice as `#pain`'s. The knob looks exactly as it does today: the screen's edge already cuts off those 6px, and the section's edge is in the same place. `clip` rather than `hidden`, so `overflow-y` stays visible and the tags that sit 13px above the comparison, and the knob's shadow, are not trimmed. Smallest change, no visual difference. Tried during triage as an injected style, 390px touch phone: the page stayed exactly the screen's width through the whole hint, sampled every frame, and the tag above the comparison still showed.
- **Option B: keep the whole knob on screen.** Stop the seam's travel, or the knob, at least 26px (28.3px scaled) in from the screen's edge at narrow widths, so the knob is never cut. This shows a whole knob where today it is cut, and the seam no longer reaches the comparison's very end on a phone. A visible change; only if the founder prefers it.

Either way, stepping the seam with the arrow keys and dragging it must stay free all the way to both ends — the comparison's words turn over by where the seam is, and a seam that can't reach the end leaves the last step unturned.

**Tests to expect:**

- A new test, beside the card decks' sideways check in `tests/e2e/home-card-decks.spec.ts` or in `tests/e2e/home-before-after.spec.ts`: at 390px with motion on, scroll `#ba` into view, sample `sidewaysOverflow(page)` (`tests/e2e/geometry.ts`) every animation frame, or every ~50ms, for 3 seconds, and assert it never exceeds 0. The sampler has to be in the page (`requestAnimationFrame`) or fast: the window is about 600ms, so a 250ms poll can miss most of it. Also drag the seam to each end and assert 0 while held and after release.
- `tests/e2e/home-calculator.spec.ts`: the `expect.poll` in `readSliderPixels` that waits for the page to fit the window was written for this bug; with it fixed, it should pass first time. Remove it or say why it stays.
- The Reference comparisons for `#ba` measure positions, sizes and colours, not `overflow`, so they should be unaffected by option A.

No ADR: the change is easy to reverse and its reason is plain, so a comment where the rule lives covers it.
