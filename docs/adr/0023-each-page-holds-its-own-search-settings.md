# Each page holds its own search title and description

Ticket 26 gave the six marketing pages a search title, a description and a sharing image an Editor can change, and put all six pages' on one entry of its own, **الظهور في البحث والمشاركة**, rather than on each page's entry. That went round a trap, with the founder's agreement on 20 September 2026: adding a field to a page's entry broke every database built from scratch. Ticket 63 has since closed the trap, and left moving the fields as the founder's to want.

One entry turned out to cost more than a second place to look. An entry is published in English as a whole, so no page could be published in English until the English search titles and descriptions of all six were written. The founder chose on 24 September 2026 to move them (ticket 91):

**Each page's search title, description and sharing image are the last tab of that page's own entry**, also named **الظهور في البحث والمشاركة**, beside the page's other words. They are published with the page, in the page's languages, and a page can be published in English on its own. The entry that held all six is gone.

## Consequences

- **The move keeps what visitors see.** A migration carried the words onto every version of each page's entry, in both languages: a published version was given them as they were last published, a draft as they were last saved, so a draft waiting to be approved kept its search title's English. No page became English by the move: a published page left without its search title's or description's English had English taken off its languages, since it was a notice before. A draft of the old entry is carried only onto a page that had a draft of its own to carry it; a page with nothing waiting keeps its published search words. The old entry's tables were then dropped. How the data moved between two schema changes is in `src/migrations/search-settings-move/statements.ts`.
- **An English page's search title is checked with its other words.** Ticking English on a page's entry asks for its search title's and description's English along with every other word, and the CMS names an empty one when Publish is pressed.
- **Whether a page is in English is decided by its entries, listed once.** `src/content/pages/page-entries.ts` lists the entries each page reads, and `src/content/pages/languages.ts` asks them, without building the page, for the sitemap, `llms.txt`, the language switcher and an English address's notice. The table in `docs/deployment.md` that tells the founder what to publish is held to that list by `tests/unit/page-entries.spec.ts`, and `tests/e2e/english-pages.spec.ts` publishes each page's English by it.
- **A marketing page's date in the sitemap is its own entry's.** It was the shared entry's, the day any page's search settings were last published. It is now the day the page's entry was last published, which covers its search settings and its words. The blog index, the legal pages and the case studies index, which carried the shared entry's date though it described none of them, now carry none.
- **The blog's and the case studies' index lines are unchanged.** They were never on the shared entry: they are `index-leads`, which is published in both languages.
